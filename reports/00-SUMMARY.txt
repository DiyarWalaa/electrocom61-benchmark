PASTE THESE FIRST

  Three checks returned DIFFERS. Each names a specific correction or addition:

  1. C08-training-configuration.txt
     The hardware. Both papers print "The P100 Kaggle GPU is used for the training." The
     word "Tesla" appears in neither. Anywhere the manuscript or data/config_provenance.csv
     cites "Tesla P100" to these sources, it is attributing a word they do not print.
     Also: the "Sec. IV" citation is right for the optimiser block but wrong for the
     640 x 640 resolution, the 70/20/10 split, the 61-class count and the augmentation list,
     all of which are in Section II, and for Gaib's 3-fold cross-validation, which is in
     Section III.

  2. C07-variance-and-statistics.txt
     The variance estimates and the resampling procedure are in different papers. Elhenidy
     prints 21 standard deviations and 21 confidence intervals and states no number of runs,
     seeds, repetitions or folds anywhere in its six pages. Gaib, which drops every variance
     column while reproducing Elhenidy's central values to the decimal, is the paper that
     states 3-fold cross-validation with k = 3 and three validation runs.

  3. C06-map50-95.txt
     Neither benchmark paper reports mAP@50-95 as a number. It appears in both only as an
     Ultralytics training-curve panel title, and in Gaib additionally as one sentence with no
     value attached. Saying the prior work reports mAP@50 without mAP@50-95 is right in
     substance; naming where the metric does appear makes the claim survive a reader who
     opens Gaib at page 5 and sees "mAP50-95" in the text.

METHOD

  Text layers extracted with pdftotext (-layout and -raw), split one file per PDF page so
  every quoted value carries a page index. Searching was done with a purpose-written helper
  rather than ripgrep, because reports/.scratch is both hidden and gitignored and ripgrep
  will not descend into it.

  Every table transcribed in these reports was ALSO read off a 300 dpi PNG render of its
  page, per the working rule that a two-column PDF's text layer is not trustworthy. Where
  the two disagreed, the image won. Three such disagreements were found, all in Sayeedi's
  Table 1 and all pdftotext artifacts on Data in Brief's digit spacing: "10 0 0", "12,0 0 0"
  and "11,0 0 0" where the page prints 1000, 12,000 and 11,000. No disagreement was found in
  any results table.

  Renders live in reports/tables/ and are gitignored per CLAUDE.md: they are page
  reproductions of copyrighted papers and this repository is public. The reports cite page
  and table numbers instead, so the evidence trail survives without them. Re-checking a
  transcribed value in a fresh checkout means re-rendering the page.

  Intermediate text dumps and the two helper scripts live in reports/.scratch/, newly added
  to .gitignore for the same reason.

  No package was installed. pdftotext and pdftoppm were already present, supplied by the
  MiKTeX installation at C:\Users\athar\AppData\Local\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64.
