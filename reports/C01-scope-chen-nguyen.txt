FINDINGS

1. ElectroCom61 appears in [CHEN] exactly twice: once in the introduction as a related
   dataset (PDF p. 2, printed "2 of 19", Section 1), and once in the reference list as
   reference [7] (PDF p. 17, printed "17 of 19"). Both are category (a), a citation in
   related work. Neither is (b) a dataset used for training or evaluation, nor (c) a
   comparison baseline.

2. [CHEN] evaluates on a dataset it builds itself. Section 3 states that 1610 raw images at
   3072 x 4096 resolution were gathered with a self-built image acquisition box (PDF p. 8,
   printed "8 of 19"), forming Dataset I. Datasets II, III and IV are augmentations of those
   same 1610 images (PDF pp. 10-11). Every results table in the paper is computed over
   Dataset I / II / III / IV. ElectroCom61 supplies no images and no numbers.

3. ElectroCom61 appears in [NGUYEN] exactly twice: once in Section 2 "Background" as one of
   four named example datasets (PDF p. 2, printed 2), and once in the reference list as
   reference [1] (PDF p. 8, printed 8). Both are category (a). Neither is (b) or (c).

4. [NGUYEN] evaluates on IoTKITs, the dataset it introduces. Section 4.4 "Training setup"
   states that each model was trained on the IoTKITs dataset (PDF p. 7, printed 7), and
   Table 4 on the same page is captioned "Training results of 4 models". ElectroCom61 is
   not among the data any row was computed on.

5. Plainly stated: NEITHER [CHEN] NOR [NGUYEN] REPORTS ANY DETECTION OR CLASSIFICATION
   RESULT COMPUTED ON ElectroCom61.

6. Incidental, and worth knowing before the bibliography is finalised: [NGUYEN]'s reference
   [1] miscites the ElectroCom61 paper. It gives the venue as "Sci. Data 11 (1) (2024) 811"
   and the lead author as "M.A.S. Faiyaz". The paper is Data in Brief 59 (2025) 111331 and
   the lead author is Md. Faiyaz Abdullah Sayeedi. [NGUYEN]'s reference [2] carries the same
   "Sci. Data 11 (2024) 811" string, so the two entries appear to have collided.

7. Also incidental: [CHEN]'s reference [7] is correct in every field checked -- authors,
   title, "Data Brief 2025, 59, 111331".
