Two points of drafting hygiene follow from the transcription:

  - When the manuscript places Sayeedi's 95.9 next to Elhenidy's or Gaib's 84.9, it is
    placing a value printed with a "%" sign beside values printed bare. Both are
    percentages, but only one paper says so in the table. If a subsection states a shared
    basis, this is the place to state it.

  - Sayeedi heads the column "mAP@50" and the two benchmark papers head it "mAP@0.5".
    data/published_accuracy.csv normalises all three to "mAP@50". That normalisation is
    correct but is not visible in the CSV; a reader re-checking a row against the PDF will
    see a different header string than the one in the file.
