COULD NOT DETERMINE

Whether the seven shared rows in [GAIB] were recomputed and happened to reproduce
[ELHENIDY]'s central values exactly, or were carried over. Nothing printed in either paper
states which. Both papers describe the same training configuration and the same dataset,
and [GAIB] additionally states a 3-fold cross-validation that [ELHENIDY] does not (see C07
and C08), which would ordinarily be expected to move at least one of 35 values. The fact of
the match is recorded; the cause is not determinable from the PDFs.

IMPACT ON THE MANUSCRIPT

No change required to the row counts or the values; the manuscript's description is
accurate as written.

Two refinements are available if Section 2.4 wants them, both of which are facts rather than
inferences:

  - "Identical values" is exactly right for the 35 shared numeric cells, but the two tables
    do not print the same THING in columns 4-6. Elhenidy prints a mean with a standard
    deviation and a 95% confidence interval; Gaib prints a bare number under a bare header.
    If the manuscript says the later paper reproduces the earlier table, saying that it
    reproduces it without the variance estimates is more precise, and it is already the
    wording used in the note on data/published_accuracy.csv row 13.

  - The shared caption names YOLOv12 in both papers. Any sentence that describes Gaib's
    table as a YOLOv13 comparison table should be checked against that, since the printed
    caption does not say so.
