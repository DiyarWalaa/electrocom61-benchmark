IMPACT ON THE MANUSCRIPT

No change required. The absolute claim -- that none of the three papers reporting results on
ElectroCom61 reports a timing measurement of any kind -- is confirmed.

One sharpening is available, and it strengthens the claim rather than weakening it. All
three papers invoke real-time operation as a motivation: [ELHENIDY] and [GAIB] each assert
in their abstract that their model "achieves higher mAP and real-time inference response
than the previous YOLO versions", and both conclude that lightweight variants are "suitable
for low-power, real-time applications". Those are the strongest speed statements in the
corpus and none carries a number. If Section 6.4 or 7.4 wants to say why measuring latency
here is worth doing, the fact that the prior work asserts real-time suitability without ever
measuring it is the evidence for it, and it is stated as a fact about what is printed rather
than as a characterisation of the authors.
