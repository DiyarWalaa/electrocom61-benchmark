FINDINGS

PART 1 -- [ELHENIDY]

1. THE STANDARD DEVIATIONS AND 95% CONFIDENCE INTERVALS ARE IN TABLE I, columns 4, 5 and 6.
   The caption is on PDF p. 3 and reads "TABLE I. A COMPARISON OF YOLOV12 WITH THE
   STATE-OF-THE-ART OBJECT DETECTION MODELS". The header row is also on PDF p. 3; all seven
   body rows are on PDF p. 4. The three headers carrying variance are printed as:
     "mAP@0.5 (mean +/- SD, 95% CI)"
     "Precision (mean +/- SD, 95% CI)"
     "Recall (mean +/- SD, 95% CI)"
   Every one of the 21 cells in those three columns carries a value, a standard deviation
   after a plus-minus sign, and a parenthesised interval. All 21 are transcribed below.

2. THE PAIRED t-TEST IS IN SECTION IV BODY TEXT, PDF p. 4, left column, one sentence:
   "Statistical tests (paired t-test, p < 0.05) confirm that YOLOv12s significantly
   outperforms YOLOv8s and YOLOv9s across mAP, precision, and recall."
   That is the only statistical test stated in the paper. No test statistic, no degrees of
   freedom, no exact p-value, and no per-comparison result are given; the threshold
   p < 0.05 is the only number attached to it. The comparison set is two models -- YOLOv8s
   and YOLOv9s -- across three metrics.

3. THE NUMBER OF RUNS BEHIND THE SDs AND CIs IS NOT STATED ANYWHERE IN THE PAPER. Searching
   the full six pages for runs, repeated, repetitions, seed, trials, folds,
   cross-validation, "n =", "average of", "three times", "five times", independent,
   replicate, standard deviation and variance returns five lines, and every one of them is
   an unrelated word-stem match ("real-world", "replicate various perspectives", "replicate
   real-world situations", "practical relevance", "recall decreased"). None states a run
   count, a seed, a repetition count, a fold count, or a resampling scheme.

4. So the position in [ELHENIDY] is: 21 standard deviations, 21 confidence intervals and one
   paired t-test are printed, and the sample size they are computed over is not stated. The
   words "runs", "seed", "fold" and "cross-validation" do not occur in the paper at all.

5. A SECONDARY OBSERVATION ON HOW THE VARIANCE IS WRITTEN IN THE BODY TEXT. Section IV's
   prose does not repeat the numeric SDs and CIs from the table; it substitutes the literal
   strings. For example, PDF p. 3: "YOLOv8s ... achieves a mean mAP@0.5 of 83.6% (+/-SD,
   95% CI) but shows relatively low recall at 53.3% (+/-SD, 95% CI)". The parenthetical is
   the placeholder text "(+/-SD, 95% CI)" rather than the values 0.4 and (83.2-84.0) that
   TABLE I gives. This pattern runs through eight sentences on PDF pp. 3-4. The table is
   the only place the numeric variance appears.

PART 2 -- [GAIB]

6. [GAIB]'s TABLE I CARRIES NO VARIANCE COLUMNS. CONFIRMED. Its six headers are Model,
   Layers, Parameters (M), mAP@0.5, Precision, Recall. The mAP, precision and recall
   headers carry no "(mean +/- SD, 95% CI)" decoration, and none of its 24 metric cells
   carries a plus-minus sign or a parenthesised interval. The strings "SD", "95% CI",
   "confidence interval", "standard deviation" and "t-test" return ZERO hits in the whole
   paper.

7. YET [GAIB] IS THE PAPER THAT STATES A RESAMPLING PROCEDURE. Section III "Methodology",
   PDF p. 2 (continuing onto PDF p. 3), states: "To verify the reliability and
   generalization ability of the proposed YOLOv13 model, the 3-fold cross validation
   algorithm was used during the training phase. In this case, the entire data set was split
   into three equal parts, such that each part was used once as a validation set, but the
   other two parts were used for training. This was repeated three times, thus allowing to
   test the model on different data splits and alleviate the risk of overfitting that comes
   with one data split. Final performance indicators were calculated as the summative mean
   of the obtained results of all the three validation runs". PDF p. 3 adds: "Using a value
   of k = 3 is a reasonable trade off between the cost of computation and eliminating
   variation in evaluation."

8. [GAIB] also asserts a statistical test without any accompanying number. Section IV,
   PDF p. 5: "Statistical tests confirm that YOLOv12s significantly outperforms YOLOv8s and
   YOLOv9s across mAP, precision, and recall." This is [ELHENIDY]'s sentence with the
   parenthetical "(paired t-test, p < 0.05)" removed. No test is named and no p-value is
   given in [GAIB].

9. THE COMBINED PICTURE IS THE INVERSE OF WHAT WOULD BE EXPECTED. The paper that prints
   variance estimates states no procedure that could produce them; the paper that states a
   3-fold cross-validation prints no variance estimates, and reproduces the earlier paper's
   central values to the decimal in all 35 shared cells (see C03). Both facts are recorded
   as printed.

IMPACT ON THE MANUSCRIPT

The note on row 12 of data/published_accuracy.csv is accurate and should stand: Elhenidy
"states neither the number of runs nor the procedure, so plus_minus and the interval are
transcribed as printed rather than interpreted here." This check confirms both halves of
that sentence against the full text.

One addition is worth making to Section 2.4, because the finding is the opposite of the
natural assumption and the manuscript would otherwise be silent on it. The variance
estimates and the resampling procedure are in DIFFERENT papers: Elhenidy prints 21 standard
deviations and 21 confidence intervals with no stated number of runs, and Gaib -- which
drops every variance column while reproducing Elhenidy's central values to the decimal --
is the paper that states 3-fold cross-validation with k = 3 and three validation runs.
Stated as a fact requiring resolution rather than as an accusation: the uncertainty
estimates in the earlier study and the resampling procedure in the later one are not
attached to the same set of numbers, and neither paper explains the relationship.

If the manuscript quotes Elhenidy's t-test, quote its scope with it. It is stated for two
comparisons only -- YOLOv12s against YOLOv8s and against YOLOv9s -- across three metrics,
with p < 0.05 as the only number given. It is not a test across the full seven-row table.
