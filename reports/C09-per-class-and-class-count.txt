   (prediction samples). The Ultralytics training-curve grids in [ELHENIDY] and [GAIB] plot
   aggregate metrics against epochs, one curve per metric, not one curve per class.

IMPACT ON THE MANUSCRIPT

No correction required. The claim that no prior study publishes per-class results on
ElectroCom61 is confirmed across all three papers, including their figures.

Two consequences are worth stating explicitly in Section 2.4, because both are absences that
constrain what a comparison against prior work can mean:

  - No prior study publishes per-class AP on this dataset. Any per-class result this study
    reports is therefore the first, and has nothing to be compared against rather than
    something it agrees or disagrees with. Say the former.

  - None of the three states the number of classes its mAP was averaged over, and all three
    use confusion matrices that carry a background row and column. A published mAP@50 on
    ElectroCom61 and one computed here are therefore not known to share a denominator. That
    is a fact about what the sources omit, and it belongs beside any table that places a
    published figure next to one of ours. It compounds the partition problem: Sayeedi
    evaluates on "the validation and testing sets" without separating them, and neither
    benchmark paper names a partition at all.
