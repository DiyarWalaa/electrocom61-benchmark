IMPACT ON THE MANUSCRIPT

No correction required. Every figure the manuscript takes from this paper is confirmed
against a 300 dpi render, the three splits sum to the stated total, and the camera claim
holds in every particular including the cover glass.

Three points bear on how the dataset description should be used:

  - The paper states no augmentation, only Auto-Orient and a 640 x 640 resize, and describes
    its own preprocessing as "minimal". If Section 3 contrasts our pipeline with the
    dataset's as-released state, that contrast rests on two named steps and nothing else.

  - No seed and no training framework version are stated, and no partition is uniquely named
    for the Table 3 figures. Together these mean the 95.9 % cannot be reproduced or even
    unambiguously located from the paper alone. This is the same gap C09 records for the
    class denominator, and it is the reason the manuscript's own reporting rule exists.

  - The 5.4-inch device sits alongside two 6.4-6.7-inch devices with different sensors,
    different cover glass and a different capture stack. If the burst-aware split work or
    any capture-condition analysis groups images by device, the camera table is the printed
    evidence for how different the three actually are, and it is transcribed here in full so
    that analysis does not have to re-open the PDF.
