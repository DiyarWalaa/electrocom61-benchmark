Both author blocks were transcribed from 300 dpi renders rather than from the text layer,
because pdftotext walks an IEEE multi-column author grid in column order and interleaves the
affiliation lines of adjacent authors. The rendered images give the printed layout directly,
which is what fixes the reading order recorded above. Both reference lists were read the
same way. No text-layer/image disagreement was found in either author block or either
reference list, but the text layer's ordering of the author grid is not usable on its own
and the image is the source of record here.
