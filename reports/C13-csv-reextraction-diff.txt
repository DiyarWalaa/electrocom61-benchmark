       and [GAIB] print bare numbers with no unit anywhere in cell or header. Storing all
       three sources as bare numbers is the only consistent choice, and the two total_loss
       rows already carry a note saying they are unitless while their siblings are
       percentages.

       papers print "YOLO v8s [20]" and "YOLO v9s [24]" with a space and a reference number.
       "YOLOv12S" keeps the printed capital S. "EcoDetect-YOLOv2" is printed over two lines
       in [ELHENIDY] and on one in [GAIB].

SEARCH TERMS USED

No text search was used to produce the re-extracted values; they were read off page images,
which is the only reliable method for a two-column IEEE table whose header and body straddle
a page break. Text search was used only to locate the tables in the first place and to
confirm each paper has no second table:

  ^\s*(Table|TABLE)\s+([IVX]+|\d+)[\.\s]
  Table\s*\d
  Table\s*(2|II)\b
  50-95 | 50:95 | mAP50 | AP50          (to confirm no mAP@50-95 column exists to transcribe)
  GFLOP | FLOP | model size | MB        (to confirm no further complexity column exists)

COULD NOT DETERMINE

Nothing about the committed values. Every one of the 48 numeric cells across the two files
was located, re-read from a page image, and matched, as were all 27 table_location values.

IMPACT ON THE MANUSCRIPT

No correction required, and no edit to either committed file. This is the strongest possible
outcome for a check of this kind: two files that no script can regenerate, hand-typed from
five PDFs, re-derived independently from page images and agreeing in every cell.

Both files' .gitignore preambles warn that they cannot be regenerated and must be
re-transcribed if lost. That warning now has a second, independent transcription standing
behind it: this report and reports/reextracted_published_values.csv together record every
value, its exact printed form, its table, and its PDF page, so a future re-transcription has
something to check itself against.
